Privacy Policy for Chatter Prime

Effective Date: May 7, 2026

At Chatter Prime, we provide Instagram automation services via the Meta Graph API. Your privacy and the security of your data are our top priorities.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Chatter Prime, we may access the following information via Instagram's APIs:

  • Profile Information: Username and User ID to identify your account.
  • Messaging Data: Content of Direct Messages and Comments (only if you enable automation for these features).
  • Interaction Data: Follower status and engagement metrics required for your chosen automation flows.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly to provide the services you request, including:

  • Automating replies to messages and comments.
  • Managing keyword-based triggers.
  • Providing analytics on your automation performance.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your private messages on our servers longer than is necessary to process the automation. Users can request data deletion at any time.

To request data deletion: Please contact us at the email below or use the "Delete Account" option within the Chatter Prime dashboard.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share information with Meta (Instagram) as required to operate the app, and with our hosting providers to maintain the service.
